# Funding Data Room Checklist

Use a private data room for sensitive documents. The public repository should contain only the public versions.

## Public folder

- Mission, theory of change, and three-module product description
- Live prototype and public source repository
- Product requirements and deployment plan
- Funding ethics and donor-influence policy
- Public roadmap and milestone status
- Safety and shared-learning guidelines with a moderation overview
- Aggregate pilot results and transparency reports
- Team biographies without unnecessary personal details

## Private folder

- Legal formation and beneficial-ownership documents
- Fiscal sponsorship agreement
- Banking and tax records
- Detailed budget and cash-flow forecast
- Founder/team resumes and signed role commitments
- Partner letters and grant submissions
- Security assessment and sensitive threat details
- Data protection impact assessment and incident plan
- Contracts, insurance, and intellectual-property assignments

## Core budget workbook fields

- funding source and restriction;
- application and decision dates;
- amount requested/awarded/received;
- payment schedule;
- eligible dates and expense categories;
- matching or reporting requirement;
- responsible owner;
- actual spend and remaining balance;
- credit expiration and covered services;
- final report status.

## Community stewardship before accepting material funding

- Written conflict-of-interest policy
- Two-person approval for material expenditures
- Separate accounting for restricted grants
- Public disclosure threshold and cadence
- Sponsor non-interference clause
- Data-access prohibition for donors/sponsors
- Refund/reallocation process if a funded milestone becomes unsafe or infeasible
