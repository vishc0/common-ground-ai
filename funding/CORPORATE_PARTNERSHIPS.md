# Corporate Infrastructure and Safe-Harbor Partnerships

Corporate outreach should request bounded support—credits, security, technical review, mentorship, or a pilot partnership—not vague sponsorship. Never trade participant data, ranking influence, exclusivity, or product conclusions.

## Priority applications

### 1. Cloudflare for Startups

- **Why first:** It matches the recommended MVP stack.
- **Current offer:** Tier 3 advertises $10,000 in credits with no minimum funding; higher partner-backed tiers advertise up to $100,000 or $350,000. Covered products include Workers, Workers AI, D1, Queues, Vectorize, and R2.
- **Eligibility constraints:** for-profit, incorporated within 10 years, technology product, valid public site, public presence, business-domain email, and other program criteria.
- **Readiness gap:** incorporation, business email/domain, founder/company profile.
- **Official source:** [Cloudflare for Startups](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/).

### 2. Cloudflare Project Galileo

- **Purpose:** security support for at-risk public-interest and civil-society organizations, typically identified with partner organizations.
- **When appropriate:** after nonprofit/fiscal-sponsor status and evidence of public-interest exposure or threats. It is not a general compute-credit program.
- **Official source:** [Project Galileo](https://www.cloudflare.com/galileo/).

### 3. Google for Startups Cloud Program

- **Current offer:** $2,000 for pre-funded MVPs; up to $200,000 for early-stage startups or $350,000 for qualifying AI-first startups.
- **Use:** alternative infrastructure/AI provider, evaluation workloads, and future portability tests.
- **Official source:** [Google Cloud for Startups](https://cloud.google.com/startup).

### 4. AWS Activate

- **Current offer:** self-funded Founders tier starts with $1,000 and may reach $5,000; portfolio applicants can receive up to $200,000 through an Activate Provider; larger AI credits are invite-only.
- **Eligibility includes:** pre-Series B, founded within 10 years, paid-tier AWS account, and program-specific credit history.
- **Use:** backup deployment path, security services, model-provider portability through Bedrock.
- **Official source:** [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits).

### 5. Microsoft for Startups

- **Current positioning:** startup credits, Azure AI capabilities, guidance, and go-to-market resources.
- **Eligibility includes:** privately held for-profit software product, supported country, below Series C, and other restrictions.
- **Use:** Azure credits, responsible-AI tooling, enterprise readiness, and nonprofit/corporate introductions.
- **Official source:** [Microsoft for Startups eligibility](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/signups/).

### 6. OpenAI Researcher Access

- **Ask:** up to $1,000 in API credits for a defined safety/societal-impact research evaluation.
- **Use:** model comparison and evaluation—not unrestricted production capacity.
- **Official source:** [Researcher Access Program](https://grants.openai.com/prog/openai_researcher_access_program/).

## Outreach sequence

1. Secure domain and role-based email (`partnerships@...`).
2. Publish funding ethics, privacy architecture, public roadmap, and license decision.
3. Prepare a two-page partner brief and 90-second prototype video.
4. Apply to Cloudflare and one backup cloud program; avoid splitting the MVP across three clouds.
5. Ask responsible-AI/security teams for a bounded architecture or red-team review.
6. After the pilot, request introductions to corporate foundations and matching-gift programs.

## Partnership diligence questions

- Which products and charges are actually covered?
- When do credits expire, and what happens at exhaustion?
- Is a payment method required and can spend caps prevent surprise billing?
- Does the provider claim rights to prompts, outputs, telemetry, or derived data?
- Are inputs used for model training, and can that be contractually disabled?
- Where are data and logs stored and for how long?
- What security, incident response, deletion, and export guarantees apply?
- May the project publicly disclose support and independently publish negative findings?
- Is exclusivity required? If yes, decline unless independently reviewed.
