# Funding Strategy

Common Ground AI should fund its public-benefit core without charging ordinary participants or selling personal profiles. Use three parallel tracks:

1. **Community crowdfunding** — validate that people understand and support the mission.
2. **Grants and philanthropy** — fund safety, community research, accessibility, governance, and open public infrastructure.
3. **Corporate in-kind support** — cloud credits, AI credits, security, mentorship, and technical review.

The platform is deliberately cross-sector. Applications should use a **core platform + themed pilot** approach: align one pilot with a funder's legitimate interest while keeping the underlying mission, governance, and technical architecture independent. See the [thematic funding map](THEMATIC_FUNDING_MAP.md).

This directory is a working funding pack, not legal, tax, or fundraising advice. Eligibility, deadlines, and terms must be rechecked immediately before applying.

## Recommended entity strategy to evaluate

- **Near term:** use a transparent personal/organization fundraiser only if the organizer can lawfully receive and account for funds.
- **Grant track:** seek a nonprofit fiscal sponsor or qualified nonprofit partner. This can enable tax-deductible fundraising and eligibility before forming a standalone nonprofit.
- **Commercial track:** if paid organizational tools are later developed, evaluate a public-benefit company or a controlled earned-revenue subsidiary with counsel.
- Keep grant-restricted funds, unrestricted donations, credits, and commercial revenue separately accounted for.

Do not call donations tax-deductible unless the fundraiser is operated through an eligible nonprofit or fiscal sponsor that confirms this in writing.

## Funding sequence

| Window | Objective | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 days | Raise $25,000 community validation round | Public prototype, budget, founder identity, weekly updates, fiscal handling explanation |
| 0–60 days | Secure $2,000–$10,000 infrastructure credits | Public site, GitHub activity, domain email, entity/founder profile |
| 0–90 days | Submit 3–5 aligned grant applications | Fiscal sponsor/entity, pilot partner, safeguarding policy, measurable outcomes |
| 3–6 months | Fund a 100-person closed pilot | Safety results, usefulness data, cost per answer, letters of support |
| 6–12 months | Seek $150,000–$500,000 institutional round | Independent evaluation, governance, retention, audited budget, implementation roadmap |

## Funding narrative

> Common Ground AI is building a free, privacy-conscious public problem-solving network. People ask questions with coarse, non-identifying context; AI offers a structured proposal; communities test, challenge, and improve it. The resulting Tree of Life preserves useful solutions, evidence, uncertainty, and principled disagreement. Funding supports safety engineering, community-led pilots, independent evaluation, and open public infrastructure—not advertising or the sale of personal profiles.

## Non-negotiable donor commitments

- No donor or sponsor can purchase ranking, moderation outcomes, participant data, or favorable answers.
- Sponsorship and conflicts of interest are public.
- Restricted grants are tracked against their approved purpose.
- Aggregate research releases use privacy review and minimum cohort thresholds.
- Funding source, amount band, purpose, and material conditions appear in a public transparency report.
- Corporate credits do not create permanent technical lock-in; provider adapters and exportable data remain required.
