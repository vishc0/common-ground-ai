# GoFundMe Campaign Draft

## Recommended campaign type

Until a fiscal sponsor or nonprofit is secured, GoFundMe permits a personal/organization fundraiser that transfers to a personal or business/organization bank account. Personal-fundraiser donations are generally not guaranteed to be tax-deductible. A certified nonprofit fundraiser sends funds directly to an eligible nonprofit and may provide tax receipts in supported jurisdictions.

Confirm the organizer is in a [supported country](https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001972748-Countries-supported-on-GoFundMe) and meets [withdrawal requirements](https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001977868-Requirements-to-receive-funds) before publishing. GoFundMe currently charges no setup/platform fee for a standard fundraiser but deducts transaction fees; its U.S. example is 2.9% + $0.30 from a $50 donation. Sources: [fundraiser types](https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-us/articles/203603984-Choosing-a-fundraiser-type), [fees](https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-us/articles/203604424-Learn-about-GoFundMe-fees), and [tax information](https://support.gofundme.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039267752-Tax-information-for-donors).

## Campaign story

### The problem

Millions of people ask AI for help, but their experiences disappear into private chats. Popular forums organize attention; they do not reliably preserve why a solution worked, for whom it worked, what failed, or where people still disagree.

### What we are building

Common Ground AI is a free public problem-solving network with three parts:

1. Ask a question using broad, non-identifying context.
2. Explore and improve questions and answers from other people.
3. Connect proven ideas, evidence, and respectful disagreement into a living Tree of Life.

AI provides a transparent starting point—not a final verdict. People can agree, disagree, pass, add evidence, and report outcomes. Majority opinion never automatically becomes truth.

### Why support is needed

The prototype exists and its source is public. The next step is a secure closed pilot. Funding will pay for privacy and safety work, accessible product development, responsible AI testing, community research, and basic infrastructure.

### The promise

Core participation will remain free. We will not sell personal profiles or private question histories. Sponsors cannot purchase favorable answers or control community conclusions. We will publish milestones, spending summaries, and lessons—even when an experiment fails.
